Brand: {company}
Target channel: {channel}
Issue: {issue}
Reason: {reason}
Rule to follow: {rule}
Flagged text: "{text}"
Surrounding context:
"{context}"

Requirements:
1. Provide exactly 3 diverse, professional alternative phrasings that replace the flagged text cleanly in context.
2. Every alternative MUST strictly adhere to the brand rule and eliminate the flagged issue.
3. Keep the alternatives natural and appropriate for {channel}.

Return ONLY a JSON list of 3 strings, for example:
["Alternative 1", "Alternative 2", "Alternative 3"]
