NovaCart Official Brand Guidelines (2026 Edition)

1. About Us & Core Facts
NovaCart was founded in 2019. We are an e-commerce platform that currently serves over 14,000 active customers worldwide. Headquartered in Boston, our mission is to empower independent merchants.

2. Brand Voice & Tone
Our tone is professional, trustworthy, and friendly but factual. Avoid excessive hype, artificial urgency, or overly casual slang.

3. Terminology & Prohibited Vocabulary
- Never refer to people who shop with us as "user". NovaCart calls the people it serves "customer" or "member".
- Do not describe our pricing or goods as "cheap". Use "affordable" or "cost-effective" instead, because cheap implies poor quality.
- Avoid claiming any results are "guaranteed". Use "designed to" instead, as outcomes depend on many external factors.

4. Claims & Compliance Policy
No unsupported superlatives (such as "#1", "best", "leading", or "revolutionary") and no guaranteed financial results unless backed by verifiable third-party evidence.

5. Approved Messaging
- Tagline: "Built for growing retailers" (approved for all marketing collateral and website headers).
- Mission Statement: "Simplifying modern commerce for independent merchants."
